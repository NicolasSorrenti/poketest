# Neo v0.52: Genesis Power migration

Five historically printed Neo Genesis Powers implemented: Feraligatr Berserk and Downpour; Typhlosion Fire Recharge and Fire Boost; Noctowl Glaring Gaze. Includes once-per-turn and repeatable limits, mandatory on-play milling, private hand inspection, card conservation, explicit targets and shuffled deck search. Five printings added to curated Neo decks. New focused tests are part of npm test. Other missing Powers and Trainers remain outside this milestone.

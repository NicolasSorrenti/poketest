# Neo v0.75 — Misty's Psyduck ESP copied extra Energy costs

Misty's Psyduck ESP (Gym Challenge) now permits executable Energy-discard attacks only when the Psyduck has enough attached eligible Energy to satisfy the additional printed discard. After all three ESP coins are heads, the player chooses a Defending Pokémon attack, then chooses and discards exact attached Energy cards in a separately authorized transaction. Rainbow Energy qualifies for typed Fire requirements, with full card conservation and one-time payment even across optional Sabrina's ESP reroll decisions. Psyduck retains its own printed Energy attack cost. With no payable/implemented copied attack, a triple-head ESP resolves as a no-effect attack instead of rolling back for unlimited retries; incomplete copied scripts remain gated. Tests exercise Charizard Fire Spin, payments, ownership, type restrictions, no-action and no-handler outcomes.

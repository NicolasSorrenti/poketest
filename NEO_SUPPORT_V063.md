# Neo v0.63 — three previously missing Trainers

Super Scoop Up flips once, then on heads returns one selected in-play Pokémon, its evolution cards, all Energy and all Tools to the owner's hand; if Active was selected, the existing promotion decision is used. Balloon Berry (Neo Revelation, corrected ruling) sets Retreat Cost to 0 and is discarded on a successful retreat in place of an Energy payment; a failed Confusion retreat does not consume it. Magnifier attaches as a Pokémon Tool, ignores defending Resistance for its host's attacks, and is discarded at the end of its owner's turn, including from the Bench. All three are routed through the existing CPU/online reducer. Verified in focused scenarios plus the full CI suite; historically rare cross-Stadium modifiers remain worth testing.

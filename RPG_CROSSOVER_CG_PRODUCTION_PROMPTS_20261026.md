## Shared production standard

Create a **single cinematic Japanese visual-novel / JRPG event CG** for each entry, 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080 or higher, export WebP at listed exact repository path. It must look like a beautiful full-scene key illustration from a modern premium anime game, **not a comic page, collage, panel grid, manga strip, character sheet, or portrait lineup**. Rich painterly anime rendering, anatomically coherent hands, excellent background perspective, authentic expressions, thoughtful gesture, strong composition, and distinct shot design across sequential scenes. Keep the bottom 20% clear of essential storytelling detail for dialogue overlay. No text, logos, watermark, labels or subtitles in images. Reference each person's approved in-repository portrait bible; avoid drift in face, hair, proportions, tattoos or costume. **Adult characters only for suggestive styling**; for unknown ages use entirely nonsexual SFW styling. Romantic/fanservice energy may be conveyed through expressive eye contact, fashion styling, confident posing, sunlit skin and playful romantic tension, but no nudity, explicit exposure, or voyeuristic framing. Respect the event script and wardrobe; if the event is a swimsuit design consultation, the swimsuit appears in the sketch, not worn. Do not reuse prior table layouts, poses, angles, or compositions. Each result requires human visual approval before `artPending` may become false.

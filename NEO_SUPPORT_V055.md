# Neo v0.55 — six historic Trainers

- Mary: draw two first; owner chooses exactly two other cards for a shuffled return.
- PokéGear: look at top seven; optionally obtain one Trainer; shuffle and lock further Trainers this turn.
- Super Energy Retrieval: trade two other hand cards for exactly four basic Energy, or all if fewer exist.
- Time Capsule: opponent then current player can each choose five eligible discard cards, or all if fewer, or decline; individual decks are shuffled and further Trainers blocked this turn.
- Pokémon Personality Test: owner conceals a selected Evolution until opponent chooses Light/Dark/neither; correct guess draws three for opponent, otherwise owner draws.
- Impostor Professor Oak's Invention: owner privately inspects opponent Prize identities, optionally shuffles them into opponent deck and sets the same number of fresh Prize cards.
- All choices use server-owned decisions and exact owner permissions. Each print joins curated Neo pool. All original Gym/RPG content is retained.

Scope: this update does not claim full Neo Trainer coverage or verify every historical ambiguity.

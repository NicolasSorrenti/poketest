# Jicy — Character Bible

## Core identity

Jicy is a 22-year-old adult and an optional romance heroine. She is genuinely sweet, attentive, and eager to help. She notices small needs, remembers what people tell her, and is happiest when she can make someone's day easier. Her warmth is sincere; she never uses kindness as leverage.

## Private truth and public behavior

Jicy is secretly a defender. This fact is author-only for this first story arc: no scene, dialogue, CG, or narration in the five main stories or optional events may reveal that she wants to fight, explain why she is prepared, or show her in combat. In public she is simply friendly and welcoming. Keep the secret available for later game planning without teasing or confirming it here.

Do not include public reactions to her outfit in this route or its random events. The player may briefly notice the natural movement of her body when she makes one of her quick excited hops, but the scene should move back to her expression and personality immediately. Do not turn this into a recurring gag or make other characters comment on it.

## Romance arc

Jicy develops a crush on the protagonist at first sight during their first meeting at the card shop. She is too shy to name it, so she turns the feeling into practical offers to help. Over five main events she moves from nervous enthusiasm, to comfortable companionship, to honestly asking for a date. The player can accept, take the relationship slowly, or choose friendship; Jicy receives each answer with warmth and respect.

Her mannerisms are recurring but subtle: a slow side-to-side sway while she talks or listens, followed by small quick hops when she gets excited or nervous. She is aware that she does this and can laugh gently at herself. These movements are a personal quirk, not a clue that exposes her secret role.

## Voice and scene-writing rules

- Keep her dialogue kind, earnest, gently self-conscious, and specific. She can ramble when nervous, then catch herself and laugh.
- She offers help, but accepts a no and learns to spend time with the protagonist without needing a task.
- Her crush appears through attention, blushes, remembering details, and brave little invitations. She does not confess until the fifth main story.
- The five route scenes and random encounters feature Jicy and the protagonist only. Do not make the random encounters about her outfit or use cross-character cameos.
- Keep TCG references grounded in deck-building, favorite cards, and friendly play. Do not replace her social arc with a battle plot.
- Do not make her naïve, helpless, robotic, futuristic, or single-mindedly focused on combat.

## Visual lock

Use the approved compact adult proportions: shorter leg-to-torso ratio, narrow waist, and the established chest and hip proportions. Her hair is blonde, very straight, and gathered in a slim high ponytail centered on the crown. The main side fringe sweeps to image-right; one separate, slightly wider fine lock sweeps toward image-left and ends at lower-lip level. No face-framing side strands. Her eyes are large and round rose-pink, with long lashes, fuller brows, and fuller lips.

Her canonical outfit is the clean sleeveless hot-pink combat suit: high zip collar, broad charcoal band under the chest, widened arm openings, high-cut mobility construction, fitted pink leg coverings, black thigh bands, practical pink-and-black boots, and one small hip deckbox. Keep it consistent and uncluttered. No jacket, headphones, extra harness straps, heavy armor, or sci-fi details.

# Elena Crowe — Character Bible

## Role, voice and inner life

Elena is a 26-year-old adult, senior analyst at the local bank branch. Her preferred role is an optional romance heroine who appears completely self-contained. Her voice is measured, precise, and quietly authoritative. She rarely raises it and never wastes words.

She values control because uncertainty has cost her before. The polished appearance, the perfect makeup, the structured schedule — all of it is intentional armor. Underneath is someone who is more observant and more lonely than she lets on. Her arc is about allowing a single person to see the moments when the control is deliberate rather than effortless, and learning that vulnerability does not automatically equal loss of power.

## Visual direction

Unambiguously adult. Refined, elegant figure with soft curves that tailored clothing still flatters. Long dark brown hair kept immaculate. Deep hazel-green eyes that hold attention. Full lips with carefully applied lipstick that never looks rushed. She is always in office attire or a very close variation of it. The attractiveness is quiet, controlled, and deliberate rather than flashy. Match Neo Harbor’s clean anime/JRPG style with polished lighting and restrained color.

## Gameplay hooks

Uses the existing `power_reserve` deck. Arrives on Day 10. Schedule is heavily centered on the bank (center) during the day and quiet locations in the evening. Five sequential chapters that slowly chip at her composure.

## Speaking guidelines

She is polite but distant at first. Compliments are rare and therefore carry weight. When flustered she becomes even more precise rather than flustered in an obvious way. Eye contact is intentional; she notices when the player holds it. Never make her suddenly bubbly or overly emotional. Softness should feel earned and slightly dangerous for her sense of order.

See `art_manifest.json`. Portrait should sell the composed beauty, perfect makeup, full lips, and especially the eyes. Clothing remains professional. Event images can show small cracks (blazer off, one extra button undone) but never a full departure from her controlled image.

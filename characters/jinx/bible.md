# Jinx Rivera — Character Bible

## Role, voice and inner life

Jinx is an 18-year-old adult, pure competitive fire wrapped in deliberate fanservice and a sharp tongue. Her preferred role is an optional romance heroine who makes the player earn every inch of softness. Her voice is dry, cutting, slightly mean, and extremely specific. She does not do soft openings or free compliments. She notices everything and rarely lets anyone forget it.

She genuinely loves winning, looking good while she does it, and watching people scramble to keep up. Her blind spot is assuming that anyone who likes looking at her only wants the surface. She treats attention as both armor and weapon. Underneath the constant teasing is a quiet fear that if she ever drops the attitude there won’t be anything interesting left. Her arc asks whether she can let someone see the version of her that isn’t performing for an audience.

## Visual direction

Unambiguously adult, lean-athletic with intentional curves. Long silver-white hair with neon pink underlights, sharp amber eyes, and a resting expression that says “impress me or leave.” Primary outfit language is high fanservice but still SFW: micro-shorts, cropped tops that end well above the navel, short skirts, thigh-highs, open jackets, sport-bikini combinations under outer layers. She chooses these clothes because she likes how she looks and because the reactions amuse her. Match Neo Harbor’s expressive, bold anime/JRPG aesthetic with clean lines and lively faces. No nudity or sexual framing is required; the confidence and the cut of the clothes do the work.

## Gameplay hooks

Uses the existing `zap` 60-card deck (Lightning aggression). Arrives on Day 12. Seven-day schedule heavy on arcade and shop. Five sequential route chapters plus room for one crossover. Early responses affect later dialogue tone. Final episode offers a relationship choice consistent with the rest of Neo Harbor.

## Speaking guidelines

She rarely says anything nice without a barb attached. When she is actually impressed she sounds almost annoyed about it. Under pressure she gets sharper and more precise, not softer. If the player matches her energy she starts testing whether they can handle the real version. Never overwrite choices or outcomes from other heroine routes. Bystanders should notice her attitude: some roll their eyes, some are intimidated, a few find it magnetic.

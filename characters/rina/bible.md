# Rina Hoshino — Character Bible

## Role, voice and inner life

Rina is an 18-year-old adult, rising local idol who still does weekend stage shows and small events. Her preferred role is an optional romance heroine whose public persona is bright and polished, but whose real heart belongs to Pokémon. Her voice is energetic, a little breathy, and becomes genuinely passionate the moment the topic turns to cards, evolution lines, or her favorite Grass-types.

She loves the stage and the fans, but the idol work is still partly performance. The TCG is the one place she never has to act. Her arc is about finding someone who sees both sides of her — the sparkling idol and the serious player — and realizing she doesn’t have to keep them completely separate.

## Visual direction

Unambiguously adult 18-year-old. Bright, youthful idol silhouette with soft curves, twin tails, short frilly skirts, cropped tops, and lots of pink and sparkle. Large aqua eyes that light up constantly. She should look like she belongs on a small concert stage and also at a tournament table. Match Neo Harbor’s colorful anime/JRPG style with high energy and clean pastels. No nudity.

## Gameplay hooks

Uses the existing `overgrowth` deck. Arrives on Day 7. Schedule is heavy on arcade and park (places she can be energetic). Five sequential chapters that move from “idol who also plays” to “player who also happens to be an idol.”

## Speaking guidelines

She is naturally cheerful and a little theatrical. When talking about Pokémon she becomes more focused and sincere. She blushes easily when someone treats her hobby as seriously as her career. Never make her cynical about idol work; she enjoys it. The soft moments come from being accepted as a real trainer first.

## Art handoff

See `art_manifest.json`. Portrait and expressions should sell the bright idol energy and the genuine sparkle when she talks cards. Stage outfits are allowed and expected. Keep everything SFW and colorful.

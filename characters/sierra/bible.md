# Sierra Holt — Character Bible

## Role, voice and inner life

Sierra is an 18-year-old adult, professional bikini model by necessity and dedicated TCG player by choice. Her preferred role is an optional romance heroine who is used to being looked at but starved for being listened to. Her voice is dry, a little guarded, and becomes noticeably warmer and more animated the moment the conversation turns to decks, matchups, or card history.

She is not shy about her body — she gets paid to show it — but she is deeply protective of the part of herself that actually cares about something. Most people only ever meet the model. Her arc is about finding someone who is more interested in her disruption package than her measurements, and learning that she doesn’t have to keep the two halves of her life completely separate.

## Visual direction

Unambiguously adult. Extremely slim and long-legged with a flat chest / tiny breasts and a large, round, heavy ass that dominates her silhouette. Narrow waist, athletic but soft lower body. Long dark brunette hair, sharp hazel-green eyes, and a resting expression that looks slightly bored or unimpressed until she starts talking about the game. In swimwear she looks like a professional model. In casual clothes the same proportions are still obvious. Match Neo Harbor’s expressive anime/JRPG style. No nudity.

## Gameplay hooks

Uses the existing `blackout` deck. Arrives on Day 18. Schedule mixes park, café, arcade and shop (she often goes straight from shoots to the arcade). Five sequential chapters focused on the gap between her public image and her private passion.

## Speaking guidelines

She is used to surface-level attention and therefore tests people. Early dialogue is cool and a little dismissive. Once the player proves they care about the game (or about her beyond the body), she opens up and becomes more sarcastic, competitive, and genuinely engaged. Never make her ashamed of modeling; she is pragmatic about it. The soft moments come from being treated as a player first.

# Sol Varela — Character Bible

## Role, voice and inner life

Sol is a 26-year-old adult, extroverted spontaneous event host and aspiring community organizer. Her preferred role is an optional romance/friendship heroine who invites the player to go on adventures. Her voice is impulsive, irreverent, gently flirtatious and hyper-specific in its humor. She should not become a one-joke swimsuit character or a generic shy girl when vulnerable.

She genuinely loves lively people and wild ideas. Her blind spot is treating invitations as obligations and noise as the same thing as happiness. She believes everyone is happier when a memorable event is happening and has trouble believing she matters when the party ends. Last season she couldn't admit her waterfront festival fell apart after sponsors withdrew, instead calling it postponed. Her arc asks if she can honestly rely on others, accept a "no", and still enjoy quiet company.

## Visual direction

Unambiguously adult, confident curvy-athletic woman with long coral-pink waves, golden highlights, sea-green eyes and freckles. Primary outfit: asymmetric turquoise/coral bikini worn with open tropical shirt, bangles, practical sandals and a waterproof deck-case strap. In town she layers it with shorts, varsity overshirts, jackets and weather-appropriate coverups; she chooses daring swimwear because she enjoys it. Match Neo Harbor's expressive, bold SFW anime/JRPG aesthetic, with stylized proportions, lively expressive faces and a consistent silhouette. No nudity or sexual framing is necessary for her fanservice comedy.

## Gameplay hooks

Uses an existing `hot_water` 60-card deck, not an invented special rule. Arrives on Day 8, has a seven-day schedule and five mini-hangout scripts. Seven authored events: five sequential chapters and two crossovers with Alma and Marina. Early responses affect later dialogue, and the final episode offers a relationship choice consistent with the rest of Neo Harbor.

## Speaking guidelines

She laughs at herself more than other people. She asks the player to join ridiculous challenges but learns to respect declining. Under pressure she gets more specific and fidgety, not suddenly poetic. Bystanders have their own opinions: Yuna notices misleading invitations, Alma resists spectacle and Marina emphasizes physical safety. Never overwrite the choices or outcomes from other heroine routes.

# Luna Quinn — Character Bible

## Role, voice and inner life

Luna is an 18-year-old adult, soft-spoken, earnest, and chronically clumsy. Her preferred role is an optional romance heroine whose appeal is completely unintentional. Her voice is gentle, apologetic, and a little scattered. She never tries to be seductive. She is simply built like a goddess and has zero awareness of it.

She genuinely wants to be helpful and competent. Her blind spot is that her body constantly sabotages her efforts in the most revealing ways possible. She trips, drops things, stretches, bends over, and adjusts her clothes without ever noticing how much skin or cleavage is on display. When people stare she assumes she has food on her face or that her glasses are crooked. Her arc is about slowly realizing that the attention she receives is not always about her personality, and learning that she can still be soft and kind even after that realization.

## Gameplay hooks

Uses the existing `water_blast` deck. Arrives on Day 15. Schedule favors library, café, and shop. Five sequential route chapters focused on her clumsiness creating increasingly hard-to-ignore moments, plus optional crossovers. Early responses affect how embarrassed she becomes later.

## Speaking guidelines

She apologizes constantly. She is easily flustered but never mean. When the player points out something revealing she becomes bright red and tries to fix it in the most ineffective way possible. Under pressure she gets quieter and more careful (which somehow makes the accidents worse). Never make her suddenly confident or aware in a seductive way. Her charm is the total lack of awareness.

# Neo v0.69 — supported copied attacks
Super Metronome's and Sketch's shared copying resolver now permits audited reducer-backed kinds for defensive shields (Scrunch/Agility/Minimize), leech healing, coin-draw and Energy-scaled coin damage, plus simple lock/condition effects. When selecting an Energy-discard attack, Super Metronome only offers source attacks whose **additional** discard can actually be paid by the copier, including required Energy type. This is separate from the printed Energy cost, which Super Metronome waives. The source Pokémon remains only a choice of printed attack; effects execute as the Colorless copier. Interactive nested attacks not covered by audited paths remain excluded. Dedicated v0.69 tests validate choices, coin outcomes, shield attachment, recovery, Energy counts and typed discard gating.

# Neo v0.53 — Forretress Spikes

Both Neo Discovery Forretress printings (neo2-2 and neo2-21) are now legal in curated Neo decks. Spikes triggers when an opponent's previously Benched Pokemon becomes Active on that opponent's turn; each healthy unsuppressed Forretress contributes 10 damage without Weakness/Resistance. Tests cover multiple sources, status-disabled Powers, and excluding an owner's own turn. KO cleanup is immediate when no other decision is pending; nested mandatory-choice and intermediate multi-switch corner cases still need extended rules testing.

# Neo v0.64 — three interactive Trainers

New Pokédex (Neo Genesis 95) shuffles the deck, then lets its owner put up to five top cards in a precise chosen draw order without exposing the deck to the opponent. Energy Amplifier (Neo Destiny 98) requires a real Energy card from hand, shows it publicly, shuffles it into the deck before the flip, then optionally takes up to three Basic Energy on heads and shuffles again. Thought Wave Machine (Neo Destiny 96) flips until tails, returns the corresponding number of chosen opposing Active Pokémon Energy cards to their owner's hand, then ends the current turn. All choices are server-authoritative, replayable and owner-scoped, not client-side imitations. Ecogym and Card-Flip Game remain unimplemented. Further stress-testing is needed for extreme coin streaks and unusual concurrent card movement.

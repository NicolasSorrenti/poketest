# Neo v0.71 — authoritative copied-attack targets

Original-series Clefairy Metronome now offers legal secondary target decisions for copied Lure (attacker selects opponent Bench), Teleport (attacker selects own Bench), Dark Mind (specific damaged opposing Bench), Flitter (opponent's Active or Bench), and Whirlwind (defender chooses promotion AFTER copied damage). The defender's actual printed attack is identified with its own index and the attack resolution uses the copier's Colorless type; no printing-only shortcut is used. Unported or nested-copy scripts remain gated rather than executing as plain damage. Misty's Psyduck ESP's second-stage target validation now correctly checks its *own* Bench for Teleport, rather than the defending player's Bench. Regression tests use full reducer actions, choice authorization, and transaction rollback. Some deeper nested copied attacks remain intentionally unsupported.

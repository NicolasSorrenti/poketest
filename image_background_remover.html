Image Background Remover

Remove a flat, light background from character PNGs such as Hana, Barb, or Akari. The tool processes files in this browser and never uploads or overwrites your originals.

Your images stay on your device. Each result downloads as a new transparent PNG; the source files remain untouched.

Best for white or other mostly uniform backgrounds. The remover follows background-colored pixels connected to the image edges, which helps keep enclosed pale clothing intact. Increase tolerance for a faint halo; lower it if it eats into light-colored details.

# Neo v0.72 — attached Rainbow / Miracle Energy type fidelity

Attached Rainbow Energy counts as every Basic Energy type (but only one at a time), and attached Miracle Energy counts as every Energy type while providing two units for attack costs. An attack requiring *Energy cards of a specified attached type to be discarded* must consider these cards eligible, not merely Basic printed card names. This affects Blaine's Arcanine Firestorm and the additional-cost picker for Super Metronome's copied Firestorm. The shared authoritative helper performs the check in the initial attack cost, the chosen-payment validator, the copied-attack availability filter and its payment chooser. Tests cover two different Rainbow printings, selecting exact attached cards, excluding Double Colorless, rejecting underpayment transactionally, and preserving non-Basic status outside play. Some unusual Energy transfers and attacks affecting all attached Energy types remain open audit items.

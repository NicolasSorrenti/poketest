# Neo Campus v0.21 — Neo Discovery retail theme decks

## Newly selectable
- **BrainWave** (Brain Wave): Psychic / Water, Neo Discovery holo Espeon 1/75. 60-card historical deck.
- **Wallop**: Grass / Fighting, Neo Discovery holo Hitmontop 3/75. 60-card historical deck.

Source: Bulbapedia Brain Wave and Wallop theme deck listings, checked against the Neo Discovery card IDs. PokeBeach has a conflicting BrainWave list that totals just 58 cards and gives a different Wallop Trainer mix. This release follows the 60-card Bulbapedia lists, and tests both decks at 60 cards. Neo Revelation did not have official English theme decks.

## Engineering
- 22 new Neo Discovery printings (plus required Neo Genesis printing dependencies) added to the *curated Neo theme* whitelist and card selection UI. Neither full Neo Discovery nor Neo Destiny is unlocked.
- 31 local deck presets total (previously 29).
- Four additional Neo theme Trainer names migrated: Energy Ark, Fossil Egg, Hyper Devolution Spray, Double Gust. Choice control remains on the correct player's side; Fossil Egg includes Mysterious Fossil evolutions whose imported TCGdex record lacks `evolvesFrom`.
- Nine exact Neo Discovery attack signatures implemented: Psychic, Squeeze, Spike Barrage, Nap Time, Detect, Triple Kick, Fury Cutter, Dust Devil, and Hyper Reverse.
- Neo Baby attack prevention includes Tyrogue.

## Known incomplete interactions
These decks remain preview-grade and should not be considered historically fully verified:
- **Four attack signatures are unported**: Energy Cycle, Scout, Hatch, Magic Dust.
- Neo Discovery Pokémon Powers (including Eevee's Energy Evolution, Unown's Anger, Omanyte's Fossil) lack complete headless implementations; entries with missing imported Power name/text also require source correction.
- Broader card interaction and graphical-browser testing are not complete. The server-side duel startup and decision protocols are covered by automated tests.

A detailed per-card report is generated by `npm run audit:neo-discovery`.

## Local usage
From `neo-campus-work`, run `npm start`, then open `http://localhost:8000/classic_live.html`. Select BrainWave or Wallop. Run `npm run test:neo-themes` for the focused tests.

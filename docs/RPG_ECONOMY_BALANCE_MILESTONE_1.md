# RPG Economy Balance — Milestone 1 (October 2026)

## Goal
Preserve card-collection progression over 90 days without a ten-card booster after every regular win. TCG rules, romance, NPC difficulty, shop prices and save IDs are unchanged.

## Rules
- Settled NPC duels continue to award 15 credits for victory and 5 for defeat/draw, before existing bonuses.
- A routine victory grants one **five-card mini booster** on the first win against a particular NPC in each seven-day week, at most **two ordinary mini boosters per week**. Further wins still pay credits, points and trust.
- Authored exhibition, personal challenge, side quest and narrative duel victories award a mini booster outside that weekly cap.
- Winning the final match of any official cup grants one **ten-card champion booster** outside the cap. Earlier rounds use ordinary weekly eligibility.
- Mini boosters include one rare, one basic Energy and three other cards. Shop packs and vouchers remain 10 cards at their existing prices.
- NPC-themed reward packs use their character's expansion only after its shop unlock; otherwise the reward uses Base Set.
- Rewards are added immediately. Unopened reveals queue, survive reload and are not overwritten. The same duel receipt cannot pay twice.

## Future balancing
Before claiming the complete economy is calibrated, simulate duel-heavy, social-heavy and mixed 90-day campaigns. Character relationship milestones (68 credits + two ten-card vouchers + signature per four chapters) remain unchanged for a later pass.

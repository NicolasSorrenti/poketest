# Neo Harbor — Scene-first character interactions

This replaces the permanent 8–10-command character menu with a VN-first design. Ordinary interaction should feel like a conversation, **not a daily relationship reward collection task**.

## UX rules

- Clicking a present character opens an actual exchange with at least three lines and back-and-forth. Core authored dialogue is preserved; newer characters can fall back to their pack's voice lines.
- Ordinary Talk and lighthearted teasing do **not** grant affection, trust, attraction, credits, card rewards, or game-time progress. Repeated same-day conversations cannot be farmed.
- Most chats have **no choice**. Occasional deterministic contextual invitations offer at most two natural responses (accept/explore or decline), generated by `rpg_conversations.js`, once per character/day. Outcomes, mechanics, and reward figures are never labeled on casual dialogue choices.
- **Talk / Duel / Deck** are compact controls **outside the VN dialogue**. The Deck button only appears once character-deck collaboration has been unlocked. Clicking the character starts talking automatically; the toolbar supports explicit gameplay actions.
- Existing shared activities, appointments, dates, tournaments, bond duel trials, Deck Studio, and authored story choices remain functional. The character can occasionally invite the player into them. No more always-on lists inside conversation windows.
- Authored storyline decisions continue to matter; story choices may intentionally grant relationship progress, but **not every scene needs an explicit reward**.
- After a completed dialogue line with no available response, the dialogue can be closed; no filler 'Goodbye' menu is required.

## Relationship progression / anti-grind

- An authored chapter requiring a previous route/guest/bond chapter now asks for at most 2 affection, **in addition to** the prior chapter; casual talk is not needed to unlock the next installment.
- Final romantic commitments require 3 affection and either 1 trust or 3 completed significant chapters. An exclusive commitment still prevents committing to another character. Friendship paths remain valid.
- Planned romantic outings require 3 affection and 1 trust instead of 4/2. Situational character events use 3 affection and 1 trust instead of 5/1. Optional CG metadata tiers are 0, 1, 2 and 3 affection instead of 0, 1, 3 and 5.
- Meaningful outings and actual duels may still improve relationships. There is no expectation that a player interact with **every** NPC daily to progress a chosen relationship.
- Old saves retain their earned relationship values and all already-completed stories. No retroactive subtraction or rerolling.

## Updating characters

New JSON character packs continue to define their intro, repeat, personality, story dialogue, and authored choices. Never insert universal stat-granting menu actions into their greetings. Use organically different lines for NPC voices, and reserve consequential choice prompts for important situations.

Run `npm run test:rpg`; key regression files are `tests/rpg_conversations.test.js`, `tests/rpg_story.test.js`, and `tests/rpg_world_ui.test.js`. Additional checks cover planned dates, situations, and the CG gallery.

# Gym v0.78 — Misty's Wish exact permission effects

An approved swap with another hand card replaces the inspected Prize with a new face-down card, clearing its old Card-Flip Game reveal marker. If the opponent declines, the inspected Prize returns face-down and the player draws exactly one card. If the opponent accepts but the player has no other card to exchange, no swap and **no draw** occur. Global all-Prizes-face-up effects remain authoritative. Tests cover opponent permission, card conservation, privacy and no-spare-hand cases.

Photo Session with Barb

Take three photos of Barb. Trigger her reactions by joking, talking or walking with her, then photograph the expression before it disappears. The animation comes from the same sprite module as Daisy's rhythm game.

The demo is free play. It doesn't use your campaign save, grant rewards, or advance the RPG clock. In the RPG, invite Barb at Riverside Park through Spend time → Photo Session.

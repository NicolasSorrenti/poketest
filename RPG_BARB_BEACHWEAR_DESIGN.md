# Barb's Beachwear Design Collection — Phase 1

This batch adds **21** one-time, two-character consultations to the Neo Campus 90-day RPG. Every authorized character other than Barb gets a dedicated design scene, with a complete dialogue sequence, two choices, and a unique pending CG. These are practical collaborative design conversations: **the bikini or paired swimwear is a drawing, not a finished costume shown on a model**. No approved art or existing character portrait is replaced.

**Artwork:** All 21 have `artPending: true`, and fall back to the existing, conspicuous generic `crossover_pending.svg` until approved artwork is placed at `/rpg-assets/events/cg/season01/<event-id>.webp`. The metadata in `rpg_cross_character_events.js` provides the exact palette, construction, scene staging, outfit, and composition.

**Character agency:** The characters can request changes, different coverage, practical fit, and matching cover-ups. Jun, Teo, Elio and Nereo help with the bikini collection or matching swimwear rather than suddenly being assigned someone else's outfit or selecting a wearer without consent. The narrative is SFW and keeps everyone in their usual fully clothed outfits during this concept-design phase.

**Availability:** Encounters require both people to have been introduced, must fit the 90-day campaign, and are one time per save. Most use genuine common scheduled venues; Elena's calm fitting is an explicitly planned visit to Barb's regular café stop. No earlier romance decisions or stories are overwritten. The next phase can add **wearing/fit approval** and **beach-day payoff** scenes, each gated on completing its character's consultation, with new unique CGs.

## Approved bold beachwear styling reference (October 2026)

The **second** generated Neo Campus Beachwear Collection lineup poster is the *swimsuit construction and styling reference*, not an authoritative character identity reference. The poster labels include non-roster characters and some noncanonical visual depictions. **Do not use it to change approved character facial features, hair, proportions, gender, or personal histories.** Derive character appearance exclusively from approved visual bibles and in-repository artwork.

The ten recognizable active-roster women in the poster (Laca, Rina, Luna, Akari, Sierra, Alma, Sol, Hana, Jinx, Reina) have upgraded `beachwear.palette`, `beachwear.cut`, and `beachwear.detail` metadata. These descriptions take precedence over older simpler outfit sketches. Barb's own proposed collection aesthetic is **black and hot pink**, with asymmetrical graphic panels, choker-inspired neckline, layered hip straps, metallic ring hardware and a statement pink belt. Her own consultation event does not exist and should not be invented as part of these ten updates.

The unmatched poster labels (Nova, Mei, Kae, Yuri, Emi, Mika, Ayame, Natsuki, Taichi, Kai, Ren, Sora) are **not** mapped to other roster characters. Their established game-specific outfits remain unchanged until explicitly designed/approved. The original CG events remain design-table consultations in normal everyday clothes, with bikinis depicted in sketches. The more complex final swimsuits are intended for distinct future fitting and beach-day scenes.

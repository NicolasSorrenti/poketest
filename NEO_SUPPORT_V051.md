# Neo v0.51: copied attacks and Discovery Pokemon Powers

- Super Metronome copies validated Bench-targeting, switching and standard Energy-discard attacks with server-authoritative decisions and a preserved coin.
- Sketch waives copied Energy costs and extra payments, subject to exact previous-attacker and witness conditions.
- Ineligible copy programs are excluded from Metronome choices. Unknown programs are rejected rather than treated as ordinary damage.
- Kabuto Revive Friends: once-per-turn activated Power with a coin, optional Kabuto deck search and Bench placement.
- Politoed Frog Song: passive +40 damage after Weakness/Resistance, only on a damaging Politoed attack and if four or more Poli-family Pokemon are in play.
- Three newly selectable printings: neo2-8, neo2-27 and neo2-56.
- Adds targeted unit scenarios and refreshes card-catalog regression counts. Copy effects requiring other complex decisions remain pending.
